## Project Overview

This project analyzes airline passenger satisfaction using service quality, operational delay, and passenger demographic data. The goal is to identify the key drivers of passenger satisfaction, predict satisfaction outcomes, and translate findings into actionable airline service improvements.

The project follows a layered analytics workflow:

1. **Excel** — Exploratory Data Analysis, cleaning, and observation logging.
2. **PostgreSQL** — Data profiling, structuring, and intelligence layering.
3. **Power BI** — Visualization and business-facing insight generation.
4. **Python & ML** — Predictive modeling, feature importance, forecasting, and advanced analytics.

---

## Business Objective

To help an airline understand:

- Which passengers are satisfied or dissatisfied.
- Which service and operational factors most influence satisfaction.
- Which passenger segments are most at risk.
- Where service improvements will produce the highest satisfaction lift.
- Whether satisfaction can be predicted accurately from available data.
